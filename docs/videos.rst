GAME23: Hidden in the Fold
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. raw:: html

   <div class="kd-video"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/_mSx75r5S5Y" title="GAME23 Martin Roelfs - Hidden in the Fold" allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

Geometric Algebra Mini Event, 2023/2024. Subtitled "Geometry, Cartan-Dieudonné, Mozzi-Chasles and the Invariant Decomposition," this 77-minute lecture uses geometric experiments to build intuition around symmetry groups, matrix representations, invariant decompositions of :math:`k`-reflections, and applications to physics such as the unified Lorentz force law.

AGACSE 2021: Graded Symmetry Groups
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Applied Geometric Algebras in Computer Science and Engineering, 2021. Presented alongside his paper `Graded Symmetry Groups: Plane and Simple <https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03771>`_, this 57-minute talk dives into the algebraic machinery behind decomposing reflections and rotors in 4D spaces, including Spacetime Algebra (STA) and 3D Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA).

`Slides <https://slides.com/enkimute/graded-symmetry-groups/fullscreen>`_

Symbolic Fitting using symfit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. raw:: html

   <div class="kd-video"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/5XmFr5cFkMk" title="Symbolic Fitting using symfit - Martin Roelfs" allow="accelerometer; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

2019. Before creating the kingdon geometric algebra library, Roelfs developed `symfit <https://github.com/tBuLi/symfit>`_ (a Python wrapper around scipy.optimize and SymPy). In this talk, he demonstrates how to write clean symbolic mathematical models for constrained numerical optimization and data fitting.
